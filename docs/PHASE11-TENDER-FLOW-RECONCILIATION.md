# Dated tender-flow and shift reconciliation contract

Development only, 21 November 2026. Split posting stays disabled by default and test-only; no provider execution or production commissioning.

Report bounds are explicit timezone-aware instants converted to UTC, half-open [start,end), maximum366 days. Event date is original sale.created_at, collection.received_at, or refund sale.created_at; tender/adjustment insert time and original sale date must not relocate a later collection/refund. Stable event IDs and immutable commands mean exact replay produces no second event. Paginated detail and full bounded-window aggregates share one SQL snapshot; revenue/item-cost, tenders and collections are independently aggregated. No sale/item/tender Cartesian joins. Scope each event to its own tenant/branch/shift; a returned original from another branch does not move that branch's original revenue. Group by stored base code; never add unlike base currencies. Missing legacy currency/rate/original amounts are explicit unknowns, never reconstructed using current rates.

Sale/refund billed revenue and tax are separate from received payments. Credit origination increases debt with no received cash; collection decreases debt and adds a dated payment without revenue; debt-reduction refund decreases debt without cash payout. Split original/refund applied base amounts and original-currency budgets are authoritative. Scalar one-method transactions retain their own frozen snapshots, with incomplete provenance flagged. Existing one-method card/mobile records may be read; no new split provider/card/mobile/offline posting authority is introduced.

Cash original units: original receipt tender minus change, collection tender minus change, refund negative original allocated payout. Cash change is never revenue or debt. For each cash event expose applied base A, frozen-rate converted net N=ROUND_HALF_UP(original_net/rate,2), converted gross tender T and change C rounded separately. Allocation variance A-N and tender/change rounding variance (T-C)-N are distinct. Drawer valuation T-C = A-(A-N)+((T-C)-N). Original currency drawer units are primary physical cash reconciliation; frozen-rate base valuation is accounting disclosure, not current conversion or a provider-confirmed movement. Refunds use original leg rate and cumulative residual budget, never new FX. No rounding difference becomes a hidden fee, debt or altered allocation.

Shift cash expectations include split original tender-minus-change, positive collections and only paid split refund allocations in original units. Base applied-payment summaries include split paid/credit portions separately, never whole split total as cash; debt reductions do not enter paid-out totals. All new shift-bound cash legs/refunds must have a cash-enabled original-currency snapshot in that target open shift, else conflict before effects. Parent shift locks serialize new transactions with close; exact replay of committed commands may recover after close without posting. Closed shift identity, counts, totals, snapshot data and historical documents stay frozen. Corrections to prior closed evidence require a separate review/reconciliation result, never rewriting it. Existing queue, revocation, register, lease and proof rules remain mandatory.

Required synthetic acceptance: boundary instants/offsets, separate-date original/collection/refund, inclusive/exclusive tax and final residual, cash change and two distinct FX residuals, multiple legs/items/collections without multiplication, tenant/branch/role/revocation/expiry, exact replay, partial/final refunds, close versus posting concurrency, frozen closed reads/replays and atomic failures. Receipt/API/UI/58/80/A4 parity and software parse checks do not certify installed hardware. Split UI enablement remains gated after this work; saved quotation pricing/conversion acceptance remains separate.
