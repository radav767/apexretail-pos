# Phase 11 split tender and refund accounting contract

Development contract, 19 November 2026. No production/provider acceptance. Separate sales using different payment methods do not constitute split-tender acceptance. Existing one-method clients remain compatible until an explicitly versioned contract is implemented and tested.

## Sale, rounding and settlement

One sale, receipt, stock deduction, tax/cost snapshot and global command identity own all tender legs. Each leg has a stable UUID, method, currency, original applied amount, original tender/change, frozen exchange rate/config version and applied base amount. Base allocations must sum exactly to the final tax-inclusive sale total in base minor units. Decimal arithmetic and ROUND_HALF_UP govern currency conversion; compute taxes/discounts once for the sale, never once per tender. Allocate remaining base cents deterministically by fractional remainder then stable leg identity; persist the allocation. Reject zero/negative legs, duplicate identities, excess precision, unsupported methods/currencies and unallocated/overallocated totals. Do not silently alter quoted base allocations. Cash alone may have tender above its applied original amount and cash change in that same currency; card/mobile applied amount equals tender and change is zero. Never convert rounding residual into a hidden fee or debt.

A credit leg creates only the unpaid base portion in one receivable, requires the same customer/credit permissions and credit-limit authorization as existing credit checkout, and generates no cash inflow. Noncredit legs are applied payments; later credit collections reduce outstanding debt and add their own dated tender flow without revenue or stock. Initial paid legs are not later collections. One atomic transaction commits all legs, stock, receivable and command proof, or none. No provider success may be inferred from local persistence: provider-backed legs remain separately disabled until their intent/confirmation/reconciliation contract is accepted. Offline split capture requires a signed action/module policy and immutable queue/schema compatibility; until accepted it remains unavailable.

## Refunds and credit notes

Original item price, tax and cost snapshots are authoritative. Refund allocations use rounded cumulative returned quantity minus amounts already reversed, with the final quantity taking the exact remaining original cents. Tax-inclusive line amounts already contain tax; tax is disclosed separately, never added twice. New sale line-tax snapshots must sum to the persisted sale tax, allocating any aggregate rounding cents deterministically. Legacy positive-tax receipts without sufficient item tax proof require review; do not invent tax history.

For the existing single-credit method, a UUID-identified credit refund first reduces still-unpaid debt through an immutable credit adjustment attached to the refund receipt, with no cash/payment movement. Keep original_amount and positive collections unchanged; outstanding = original - settled - credited, bounded at zero. Expose credited separately. Refunds exceeding remaining debt, missing receivable/proof or cash-method overrides on credit originals require review before any stock or balance effect. No negative debt, fabricated payout, silent excess credit or rewritten collection is permitted. Paid/part-paid payout support is an explicit remaining gate.

For future split tenders, cap cumulative refunds by original item and each persisted tender allocation. First allocate the refund against remaining unpaid credit; allocate any paid remainder against eligible original paid legs in a deterministic persisted order, never beyond amounts applied minus already refunded. Currency payouts use frozen original leg rates and remaining original minor units; final reversal consumes exact residual original/base cents. Returning money through a different method requires current manager permission plus an explicit reviewed allocation and audit; no generic refund_method bypass. Full refund after collections may require both debt reduction and paid-leg payout, committed atomically with a distinct immutable credit note and refund allocations. Until that implementation and provider acceptance exist, reject unsupported allocations before effects.

## Identity, reporting and acceptance

Full canonical tender/allocation input belongs in the existing immutable sale/refund command proof. Same ID + exact request returns original legs/receipt before current-license/price/stock checks, retaining current actor/epoch/tenant/branch/register/revision validation; altered input conflicts. New commands need current license/modules. Concurrent equal IDs create one sale/refund; distinct commands serialize original refund and receivable budgets. Rollback retains every revocation, offline lease, original queue and proof safeguard.

Revenue/cost/tax belong to sale/refund dates. Tender inflow/outflow belongs to payment/refund/collection dates; cash change is not revenue. Historical reports must aggregate persisted legs without multiplying sales/items on joins, distinguish unpaid credit from cash and retain original currency snapshots. Current outstanding debt is not a historical as-of balance. Required acceptance: mixed cash/card/credit legs within one sale, tax inclusive/exclusive/exempt and penny residuals, multiple currencies, exact cash change, partial/final returns before/after collections, overpayment rejection, provider failure/unknown outcomes, stock invariance, API/UI/report/receipt parity, real separate-connection races and injected atomic rollback. Do not advertise split tenders until these requirements are satisfied.
