# Procurement, durable counts and document implementation contract

19 November 2026. Defines the next independent increments after quotation lifecycle. This document does not implement or certify these workflows. Existing stock/replay/authentication services remain authoritative; production and Super Admin are NO-GO.

## Procurement ownership and staged acceptance

1. Add company-owned suppliers and branch-scoped purchase-order drafts with explicit base currency, stable line identities, product/variant, ordered purchase units, frozen conversion to base units, base unit cost, version and original actor. Supplier contact fields are metadata; saving never sends email or invokes a provider. Initially only current business owners/administrators approve orders; receiving permission alone must not imply purchase approval. Any broader purchasing roles need an explicit permission/UI matrix.
2. Draft/approved/cancelled orders have no stock, sale, payment or assumed accounts-payable effects. Freeze approved quantities, units, conversion, costs, supplier and branch; revisions require a new draft. Existing physical locations are branches: do not claim a warehouse abstraction that does not exist. Purchase order total is a commitment estimate, not a paid invoice or recognized provider liability.
3. A goods receipt owns the one stock increase through existing InventoryService.receive_stock / InventoryRepository(commit_on_save=False), retaining branch/variant/batch movement and allocation hooks. Inspect variant/lot support before enabling each dimension; unsupported dimensions fail before any line is posted. Receipt and order-line received quantities, immutable command proof, audit and all stock/cost changes commit atomically. No per-line commit. No second custom stock loop beside GRN. Cost/conversion semantics reuse current inventory behavior and are recorded in the receipt, rather than inventing weighted-cost accounting.
4. Serialize company/domain/request identity before order and deterministically ordered stock locks. UUID/full-input/original actor/branch/register-revision proof returns the original receipt after expiry without reapplying stock or cost. Altered payload/purpose/context conflicts; historical unknown proofs remain read-only. New receipts need current license/modules and receiving authority. Concurrent distinct receipts serialize each approved remaining quantity; overreceipt and wrong branch/supplier/line/variant/unit fail before effects. Archive/cancel cannot discard receipt history or received stock. Existing legacy generic GRNs without IDs are not magically deduplicated.
5. Purchase returns are separate identified negative stock movements linked to original receipt line/lot, capped by received-minus-returned quantity and current available/held stock. No generic negative GRN and no sale refund substitute. A supplier credit/payment is separate accounting evidence, not fabricated by stock restoration. Missing payable/provider integration remains explicit. Until purchase-return atomicity, variant/lot traceability and financial policy are implemented, keep unsupported returns disabled.

Required first increment: supplier metadata + PO draft/approval and transactional partial/final receiving, with direct API/service, equal/different request concurrency, overreceipt, changed assignment/role/license, expired committed replay, injected multi-line rollback and cost/stock invariance. UI must expose pending outcomes/recovery before retrying a different UUID. Keep all original offline queues and movement proof identities.

## Durable stock-take sessions and movement cutoffs

Build on existing immediate counts, expected quantity and immutable count-command proof. New durable sessions have company, branch, explicit full/partial scope, frozen position keys (product/variant/lot/hold disposition), category selection and opening baseline, draft/counting/submitted/approved/rejected states, version, actors, measured quantities, variance and review history. A missing measurement is unknown, never zero. Count sheets and scan/search may not silently omit archived physical stock, variants, lots or held returns. Service/nontracked/composite-parent goods do not masquerade as independently countable physical units.

A timestamp alone is not a safe movement cutoff: created_at can precede commit, and a sale+refund can restore the same quantity. Before approval, add an authoritative per-position revision that advances for every relevant quantity/lot/hold change across all stock paths, ideally enforced at the DB mutation boundary. Preserve existing expected-quantity protection and also compare the captured revision. Baseline and measured quantity remain distinct. Any intervening movement requires explicit recount/rebase review with retained old evidence, not an invented adjustment for assumed sales during counting. Define lot/hold revision coverage before enabling batch sessions. Never compare a company-wide product total to a branch position.

Separate counting from approval: existing inventory_count does not silently grant adjustment approval; owners/administrators initially approve, with a distinct reviewed role policy before delegation. Submission/approval require current actor/epoch/branch/role and new-write license. Historical session reads and exact committed approval recovery retain current scope after expiry. Initial capture/approval is online; do not invent offline inventory rights from the signed sale/draft grant. Native disconnected count support needs its own signed action, baseline, queue compatibility and revocation acceptance.

Approval takes one command identity and atomically posts all approved variance movements through the existing count/allocation service with commit_on_save disabled, then commits the immutable session/approval proof and audit. Equal retries return original movements; conflicting payloads/versions fail. Lock command before session/ordered positions in the same order as immediate count paths. No partial approval commits, duplicate per-line stock effects, hidden zeroing or automatic deletion of a rejected/local pending count. Required races include count approval versus sale/refund/transfer/GRN, net-zero intervening movement, equal/conflicting approval IDs, concurrent approvers, missing position/changed scope, lot/hold differences and injected rollback. Draft/resume/full sheets are not complete until those guards exist.

## Safe document infrastructure

Introduce company/optional-branch template identity plus immutable versioned, schema-validated blocks. Start with safe text/table/contact/tax/terms/footer blocks and explicit paper sizes; no arbitrary HTML, script, expression, external URL, CSS injection, remote fonts or unrestricted renderer network/file access. Business logos are private tenant media with bounded bytes/dimensions, verified decode/re-encode and controlled alignment/raster rules. Approved platform branding is a separate dependency; do not treat a customer/default image as official artwork.

Render documents from existing immutable sale/refund/quotation/receipt snapshots and retained template version. Record source identity, renderer/template version and content digest for reprint/recovery. Rendering/downloading/reprinting must never create a sale, deduct stock, authorize payment, increment fiscal transactions or replace offline print-job identities. Preserve existing escaped quotation/receipt helpers while adopting the shared pipeline. Browser print/save-PDF remains user-driven output; do not label it a commissioned server PDF or physical print acceptance.

QR purpose must be explicit. Tenant-authenticated lookup or a separately reviewed minimal revocable public token cannot expose bearer/PIN/reset/handoff credentials or unrestricted customer data. Payment QR requires an approved provider intent, not a manufactured URL/success. Fiscal QR/status requires the real confirmed authority response; source preparation is never fiscalisation. Rendering tests need escaped untrusted names/addresses, cross-tenant media/template denial, resource/pixel limits, deterministic monetary snapshots, template-version reprints, zero stock/payment/fiscal changes, safe 58/80/A4 layout and scan acceptance before release claims.

Delivery is a separate consent-aware outbox with identified original document/recipient, attempts, status, sink tests and reconciliation. No real messages or provider actions under current authorization. Unknown provider outcomes retain their original ID for status/recovery; duplicate resend cannot become another financial transaction. Installed printer/WebView, PDF/QR scan, official art, vendor and fiscal commissioning stay external gates.

## Recovery invariants

Unconfirmed workflow requests keep their original UUID and complete input. Explicit cancellation serializes on that same UUID: a committed command returns its original result only with current actor/permission/register and full-input proof; an uncommitted command receives an immutable company/actor cancellation fence. A delayed original packet then conflicts permanently before posting. Cancellation cannot erase a receipt or reverse stock. Recovery remains available after expiry; a replacement is allowed only after durable cancellation acknowledgement. Purchasing records, command history and document HTML retain the existing product-cost visibility permission; blind receiving is outside the initial controls.

Every additive migration refuses destructive downgrade of new evidence. Compatible forward recovery must retain User/employee epochs, opaque/remembered/reset/handoff revocation, signed nonce/time/action leases, original register assignments, sale/refund/order/shift/stock/customer/quotation proofs, immutable documents and every original offline queue. Old API/schema fallback that ignores a new guard is not a rollback plan. Test settings are env-i synthetic/private only; no production migration, deployment, service/credential changes, messages, provider transactions or APK publication.
